# Design patterns

The primary pattern answers one question: **what organizes the square?** It is not a genre, era, visual style, rendering engine, or preset. Choose one primary pattern and one or two secondary techniques. Three initial directions must use three different primary patterns.

## 1. Portrait / Identity

A face, body, costume, or performed identity organizes attention. Decide distance, gaze, cropping, pose geometry, and whether the subject invites, resists, or ignores the viewer. Useful betrayals include obscuring the expected celebrity face or making costume and negative space carry identity.

## 2. Documentary Moment

An apparently found moment, social situation, performance fragment, or lived environment is the evidence. Preserve incidental detail, asymmetry, and temporal tension. Do not polish away the sense that something happened before and after the frame.

## 3. Narrative Tableau

Multiple figures, props, or events form a staged story. Build foreground, interaction layer, midground, background, and a directional light or geometry. The scene must contain a readable action, not just atmospheric people in a place.

## 4. Symbolic Object / Still Life

One object or a tight system of objects holds the song's contradiction. Specify scale, support surface, damage, reflection, shadow, and impossible behavior. Replace generic symbolism with a physical operation: melting, balancing, splitting, leaking, compressing, or hovering.

## 5. Typographic Hero / Wordmark

Letterforms are the main image architecture. The title may become a container, mask, path, volume, texture field, or repeated rhythm. It must survive without the supporting picture. Read `typography.md` before choosing this pattern.

## 6. Minimal Geometry / Color Field

A small number of shapes and color relationships organize the square. Define the dominant field, counter-shape, proportion, edge condition, and optical tension. Minimal does not mean empty; every millimeter and tonal boundary becomes visible.

## 7. Abstract Material / Process

The trace of a process is the subject: paint transfer, chemical bloom, folded paper, scratched emulsion, heat damage, woven fiber, liquid interference, or scanned debris. State the plausible process and what it expresses. Avoid generic digital clouds.

## 8. Archive / Collage / Found Material

Documents, clippings, labels, maps, snapshots, receipts, or fragments create meaning through selection and juxtaposition. Define hierarchy, cut logic, overlaps, scale shifts, and provenance. Use only material the user has rights to use, or generate original substitutes.

## 9. Illustration / Character World

A drawn world, character, or visual fable carries identity. Specify line behavior, shape language, surface medium, perspective, and recurring character rules. A character alone is insufficient: make the world respond to the song's specific motifs.

## 10. Landscape / Architecture / Absence

Space, built form, weather, or the conspicuous absence of a person organizes the image. Decide horizon, viewpoint, scale cue, path of entry, and what is missing. Resist the default cinematic cityscape when the track does not demand one.

## 11. Diagram / Grid / Data / Repetition

A system, sequence, index, grid, waveform, notation, or repeated unit creates meaning. Define the rule, then introduce a meaningful exception. Do not use arbitrary UI decoration or fake technical labels.

## 12. Package Object / Intervention / Anti-cover

The release behaves like an object, wrapper, warning, hole, sticker, cut, stamp, blank surface, or intervention in the package itself. The concept must remain legible in a flat digital thumbnail even if a physical edition is imagined.

## Direction construction

For each direction record:

1. primary pattern;
2. one or two secondary techniques;
3. one genre anchor that preserves recognition;
4. one genre expectation that is deliberately betrayed;
5. foreground, subject interaction, midground, background, light, and geometry;
6. color proportions, not just color names;
7. material or production logic;
8. title role and fallback typography mode;
9. what remains recognizable at 56 px;
10. why this could belong only to this release.

Reject directions that differ only by color, camera angle, or rendering style. Replace them until their organizing structures differ.
