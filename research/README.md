# Research archive

This folder preserves dated model evidence and the bounded cover-precedent
work that informs the project. It is not loaded during an ordinary cover run
and is not a menu of styles to reproduce. The runtime source of truth is
[`skills/album-cover-director/`](../skills/album-cover-director/); use research
to resolve a specific construction or execution problem.

## Current model evidence

- [GPT Image / ImageGen report, 2026-11-16](gpt-image-2-6-evidence-2026-11-16.md):
  Japanese synthesis of official 2.6 guidance and recent primary reports,
  including Reddit, with observations, limitations and project implications.
- [Source ledger](gpt-image-2-6-evidence-2026-11-16.json): publication, observation
  and check dates; model/host attribution; settings, sample and access limits;
  commercial interests, excluded sources and open questions. Community window:
  November 4–16, 2026. Live official specifications were checked November 16.
- [PR #8 review](reviews/pr-8-2026-11-16.md): immutable reviewed head/base, checks,
  regression analysis, fictional walkthrough and unverified image outcomes.
- [Runtime observation reference](../skills/album-cover-director/references/image-quality-observations.md):
  short diagnostic questions for the existing generation/inspection workflow.

These documents contain original analysis and short source summaries, not
third-party images or reproduced source articles. No image batch was generated
for this research. Reports are not general success-rate estimates, and the
ledger is a research artifact, not a production contract or automatic gate.

Keep specifications, author observations and project inferences separate. Read
source updates before treating an old incident as a current limitation. Do not
transfer historical 2.5 results in
[gpt-image-2-5-migration.md](gpt-image-2-5-migration.md) to 2.6 without evidence.

## Cover precedent work

The repository contains observations and source links only; it contains no
third-party album-cover images.

[typography-led-genre-intake.yaml](typography-led-genre-intake.yaml) is the
current image-search discovery intake. It visually screens title-led covers
across major genres and records source pages, structural observations, and
transfer questions without storing the images. Its candidates remain
discovery-only until controlled private trials satisfy the promotion gate in
`skills/album-cover-director/references/production-learning.md`.
