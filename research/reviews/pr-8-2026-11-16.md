| Part | Flat direction | Spatial direction |
| --- | --- | --- |

| Evidence still needed | Does the actual image's structure depend on the title? | Do the intended joins work at the declared 128 px view? |

Assemble the title from one canonical string, quoted once, and keep album context
out of readable cover text. The palette repeats because the fictional user
explicitly fixed it. The flat grid must not enter the spatial candidate's common
block, and the plane alignment must not enter the flat candidate. No prior
candidate's complete prompt is needed as shared input.

These are compatible instructions; the walkthrough does not establish that a
model renders either image correctly, or that either is visually compelling.
