# Provisional genre-diverse title-image intake

This is a deliberately capped six-item discovery batch. It is **not** a
production reference, a completed corpus, or evidence of cover-specific
acclaim. Each card retains a source link and one transferable title-to-image
relationship for later private cover trials. Do not add a card merely to cover
another genre; promote a relationship only after it creates a distinct,
successful generated cover.

No third-party images are stored in this repository.

| Genre | Source | Derived relationship | Prompt primitive | Reject when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Post-punk | [Savage Republic — *Tragic Figures*](https://savagerepublicipr.bandcamp.com/merch/tragic-figures-album-cover-signed-numbered-3-color-letterpress-art-print) | The exact title can be a physically printed, die-cut package object rather than a layer placed on an image. | Name the title as hand-fed letterpress ink on chipboard; limit it to two or three inks, slight registration shifts, and one die-cut aperture revealing a track-derived fragment. | It becomes a clean digital poster with a paper-texture filter instead of a made object. |
| Techno | [Cristian Glitch — *Echo Reaktor*](https://www.beatport.com/track/echo-reaktor/21067889) | Fragmented geometric modules can serve both as letterforms and as the cover's electronic structure. | Build the exact title from a limited set of broken modules; state the reading order before fragmentation and keep one solid recognition anchor in each word. | Broken forms become arbitrary sci-fi geometry or pseudo-text. |
| Brazilian dub hip-hop | [Disstantes — *Apocalipto*](https://disstantes.bandcamp.com/track/apocalipto) | A title can interrupt a scene as a damaged hand-painted sign, treated as foreground evidence rather than metadata. | Place the exact title on one distressed sign; define its tilt, tears or fasteners, and the specific image layer that may pass behind it. | It becomes a small caption, or generic graffiti replaces the sign's physical role. |
| Avant-jazz | [RogueArt visual system](https://daily.bandcamp.com/label-profile/rogue-art-label-profile) | Severe rails and active empty space can make a title system feel like the image, even with very little illustration. | Use a white field with two strict vertical rails; put title and artist in an asymmetric break on those rails and make the centre intentional silence. | It reads as an ordinary border or receives a decorative unrelated central picture. |
| Japanese indie / ambient | [Tohru Fujimura — *mild blue tone*](https://tatsutomiura.com/mild-blue-tone) | A cool physical title band can traverse an aqueous image plane and turn necessary small information into one visual rhythm. | Make one exact cool-white title band cross condensation or rain; any permitted minor characters belong to the same band, never as free captions. | The band feels like application UI, or the intended cool palette drifts into warm beige/yellow. |
| Reggaeton / dance | [*Despecho Reggaeton* cover](https://www.viberate.com/artist/hector-and-tito/) | Repeated title letters crossing a hard diagonal color collision can act as a chant or dance-floor signal. | Set the exact title once at primary scale and once as a cropped echo, both crossing one hard diagonal collision; allow no imagery beyond the title system. | Repetition obscures the official reading or the diagonal becomes merely decorative background. |

## Promotion gate

Test at most one relationship at a time in a private generated cover run. A
relationship may become a reusable production principle only if its result is
structurally distinct from the existing title map, enclosing contour, kinetic
wordmark, palimpsest intervention, and emblem orbit architectures.
